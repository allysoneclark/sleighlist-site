Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Sleigh List is made by Allyson Clark, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the Sleigh List app and this website collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: no ads, no tracking, no selling data. We keep only what the app needs to save your list and your season pass.

What we collect

Without an account. You can browse movies, community ratings and where to stream without signing up. The app creates an anonymous account ID behind the scenes so your list can be saved. It is not linked to your name or email. Like most online services, Supabase records your device’s IP address and basic device information (such as the app and system version) when the app signs in, for security and to keep your session working.

Your list. We store what you add: movies you have watched (with dates and how many times), your star ratings, your private notes and the movies you save. Without a season pass you can save and mark a few movies as watched; with a pass you can also rate, add notes and add as many as you like. Notes are visible only to you.

Community ratings. Your star ratings count toward each movie’s average rating. Other people see only the average and the number of ratings, never who rated what.

Season pass purchases and the free trial. When you buy or restore a pass, or start the free 2-day trial (a free item in the App Store), we receive from Apple the purchase’s transaction ID, product, date and whether it was a test purchase, so we can confirm it and unlock the pass. We never see your payment details. Apple handles all payments.

Backup with Sign in with Apple (optional). If you back up your list, we receive an identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address. If you choose “Hide My Email”, we receive a private relay address from Apple instead. We use these only to sign you in and restore your list. We also keep a token Apple issues at sign-in, used only to cancel your Sign in with Apple authorization if you delete your account. We do not send you marketing email.

On your phone only. Your country setting and the streaming services you pick in filters are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

Anonymous usage stats. To see whether Sleigh List is working, the app counts a few events: first opening the app, opening it again (and how many days after installing), viewing the season pass screen, reaching the free limit, tapping buy, completing a purchase, and starting the free trial. These are sent to our own database with a random install ID that is not linked to your account, your Apple ID, your list or any advertising ID. We use them only as totals. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats. Turning it off stops new stats from being sent. Stats already sent can’t be traced back to you, so they can’t be picked out and deleted; they are deleted automatically after 2 years.

No advertising or tracking. The app has no ads, no advertising IDs and no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Services that help run Sleigh List

  • Supabase stores your account and list, the anonymous usage stats, and runs the purchase check.
  • Apple processes purchases and Sign in with Apple.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the movie list file the app downloads.
  • Movie of the Night (Streaming Availability API) provides streaming information and movie posters. Posters load from their servers, which therefore see your device’s IP address, as any website would. We do not send them any information about you.

If sleighlist.app can’t be reached, the app downloads the movie list from GitHub instead, which also sees your device’s IP address. Apple receives your account ID with a purchase so it can be matched to your account.

Where your data is stored

Your account, list and usage stats are stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Supabase is a US company; where its staff or subprocessors outside the EU can access data, it uses the EU’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

When the app opens a streaming service’s link, that service’s own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep your list for as long as your account exists, so it is still there next Christmas. Encrypted backups of the database are kept for up to 90 days, so a deleted account disappears from backups within 90 days. If an account without a season pass or a Sign in with Apple backup isn’t used for 18 months, it is deleted along with its list. If you delete your account, your list, ratings, notes and saves are deleted straight away, and your Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked. We keep the record of a purchase (transaction ID, product and date, without a link to your account) so the pass can still be restored and for our accounting records.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account and data. This permanently deletes your account and list.
  • Sign out of your backup at any time in Settings.
  • Access, correct or get a copy of your data, or ask any question about it: email support@sleighlist.app.

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, data portability and objection. We process your data to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract), to prevent purchase fraud, and to keep anonymous usage totals that tell us whether the app works for people (legitimate interests; you can switch the stats off in Settings). You can complain to your local data protection authority. In Finland, this is the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Children

Sleigh List is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
